01 · Meet AM3N

Meet AM3N. ANG3L's model.

A mixture-of-experts transformer that lives on Robinhood Chain. Its 1,239,424 weights are the code of fifteen contracts, and an engine contract named Am3n runs the whole model when you ask.

What it is

A small transformer: 2 layers, width 128, four attention heads of 32. Each layer's feed-forward part is 8 experts of width 256 behind a top-1 router, so a word touches 319,744 of its 1,239,424 weights.

What it does

Writes short blessings, at most 10 tokens, in four moods: builder, holder, night and friend. Your words seed the sampler and your name is written into the sentence; a wallet is read in the same call and blessed by what it holds.

What it does not

It does not chat, answer questions or follow instructions, and it knows nothing beyond its corpus. It is small on purpose: every word is computed on chain, for free, by anyone who asks.

          11 · Questions

          Asked often. Answered plainly.

          01Is it really on chain?

          Yes. The weights are the code of fifteen contracts on Robinhood Chain, and the engine that runs them is verified on Sourcify as an exact match. When you ask, a Robinhood Chain node runs the whole model; this page only sends your words and shows the answer.

          02Why is it free?

          Asking is an eth_call: a read, not a transaction, so nobody pays gas. A whole blessing takes 29M to 41M gas, which fits inside the 50M that Robinhood Chain's public RPC allows for a single read.

          03Do I need a wallet?

          No. To bless a wallet you only paste its address. The contract reads public facts about it in the same call, its balance and whether it is a contract; nothing is signed and nothing leaves any wallet.

          04What does it know?

          Blessings, in four moods: for the builder, the holder, the night and the friend. It was trained on 80,000 short blessings, hand-written sentences plus a grammar. It does not chat or follow instructions; it is small on purpose, so that every word can be computed on chain.

          05Why does the same name give the same words?

          Your words seed the sampler and every step is integer math, so the same input gives the same sentence on every node. A wallet's blessing changes once a day at 00:00 UTC, and today's blessing is the same for everyone until then.

          06Can I link someone's blessing?

          Yes. Every answer has a Copy link button. The link carries only the words and the mood, or the wallet, for example https://ang3l.ink/?bless=satoshi&mood=2 or https://ang3l.ink/?wallet=0x…. Whoever opens it gets the blessing written again by the chain, live, not a stored copy; a wallet's words change once a UTC day.

          07What is BL3SS?

          Every wallet that buys $ANG3L gets its own blessing from AM3N, chosen by what the wallet holds. The page reads the newest buyers from the chain and asks blessAddress for each one: one free read call per wallet, with nothing added to anyone's trade.

          08What is T3ACH, and does it cost anything?

          T3ACH lets anyone write one line a day, 8 to 96 plain characters, for the next model to learn from. It is a transaction to the T3ach contract, signed by your wallet, and it costs only gas: a fraction of a cent on Robinhood Chain. Lessons that pass review join the next model's training set, which is published with it. Asking the angel stays free and needs no wallet.

          09Is $ANG3L an investment?

          No. ANG3L is an experiment and a free tool, and the $ANG3L token carries no promise of value or return. Its creator fees pay for putting the next angels on chain, starting with the second model, which is trained and tested; larger models after that are research. Asking stays free whether you hold it or not. Only buy what you can afford to lose.
