What happens when you ask

  1. Your words become a seed. keccak256 of what you typed, or of the wallet and the day, picks the path through the model.
  2. The weights are read from chain. Embeddings and attention load once; for every word a router picks one of eight experts per layer, and only that expert is read.
  3. The forward pass runs in the contract, integer math only: eight int8 weights per 256-bit multiply, a shift for every scale, an integer square root, a table for exp.
  4. It samples one word at a time from the top eight until the blessing ends, and writes your name where the model asks for it. Every step happens inside the same read call.

Other on-chain models need hundreds of paid transactions for a single word. This one answers a whole sentence for free.

Where the weights live

Each data contract was created through the standard CREATE2 deployer (0x4e59…956C, salt 0), so its address is derived from the hash of the exact bytes it holds. A different weight cannot sit at these addresses.
