What happens when you ask

  1. Your words become a seed. keccak256 of what you typed picks the path through the model.
  2. The forward pass runs in the contract. Embeddings, two attention layers and an MLP, all in integer math: int8 weights, a shift for every scale, an integer square root, a table for exp.
  3. It samples one word at a time from the top eight, until the blessing ends. Every step happens inside the same read call.
